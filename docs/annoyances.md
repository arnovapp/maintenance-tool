# Annoyances Log

Every time the tool frustrates you, slows you down, confuses you, or makes you think "I wish it did X," write it here. Timestamp it. Don't fix it for 30 days.

The rule is hard: **for the first 30 days of daily use, capture only.** Do not schedule fixes. Do not open PRs for these items. Do not even let an agent work on them.

Why: half of what feels critical in week one will not matter in week three. Your brain will habituate or find a workaround. The items that show up 5+ times in 30 days are your real roadmap. The items that show up once were noise.

At the 30-day mark, review this log. Cluster repeated items. Pick the top 3. Build those. Repeat at 60 and 90 days.

---

## Template

```
[YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM] Short title
Context: What I was trying to do
Annoyance: What went wrong, felt slow, was confusing
How I worked around it: (if applicable)
Severity: low / med / high
```
